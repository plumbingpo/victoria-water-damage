Few water damage events are as common, or as potentially devastating to a property, as a pipe that bursts. A single burst pipe can release 4 to 8 gallons of water per minute, which is 240 to 480 gallons per hour flooding through your walls, ceilings and floors. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

Victoria Restoration provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout Victoria and surrounding areas. After the water is out, we dry every material that took on moisture: finished floors, subflooring, wood framing, insulation and drywall. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Check water pressure with a gauge, since anything over 80 PSI puts stress on fittings and pipes
  • Before winter, drain outside spigots and take the hoses off them
  • Put leak sensors by the water heater and along the main supply line
  • Going away in cold months? Keep the thermostat running to protect the plumbing from freezing
  • Replace old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes before they fail unexpectedly
  • When it freezes, let taps drip and open the cabinets under sinks to let warm air reach the pipes

Signs It Is Time for Burst Pipe Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Hearing water move through the house while nothing is turned on
  • Faucets and showers across the building suddenly running weak
  • Frost or ice on exposed pipes during cold weather
  • Faucets that give only a trickle, or nothing, during a freeze
  • A hidden leak often shows up first as a much larger water bill
  • Ceilings or walls that show damp patches, stains or a swollen bulge
  • Water pooling on floors with no apparent source

Why Professional Burst Pipe Repair Matters

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength. With moisture meters and thermal cameras, trained technicians follow the water's path and pinpoint every affected area right away.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. Compared with a shop vacuum, truck-mounted extraction is hundreds of times faster, and compared with a residential dehumidifier, a commercial one removes 10 to 20 times as much moisture. Mold can begin growing in 24 to 48 hours, so this kind of power and speed matters a great deal.
