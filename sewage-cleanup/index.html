A sewage backup is one of the most hazardous situations a property owner can face. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Victoria Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Victoria, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

How to Tell You Need Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path
  • People in the household getting stomach or intestinal symptoms with no clear cause
  • Foul-smelling moisture showing up on flooring or along the walls
  • More than one drain in the house overflowing or backing up together
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs
  • Sewage residue visible around drain openings or on basement floors
  • Every sink, tub and shower draining slowly even after you plunge them

Why Professional Sewage Cleanup Matters

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. Because raw sewage is a biological hazard, the work demands trained people, protective equipment (gloves, boots, respirators and Tyvek suits) and disinfection products registered with the EPA and not sold to the public. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Have your sewer line inspected with a camera every 2 to 3 years to catch potential problems
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • Know the location and condition of your sewer cleanout for emergency access
  • A backwater valve at the sewer connection stops the city system from backing up into your home
  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs
